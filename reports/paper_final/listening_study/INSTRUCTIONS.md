# Participant instructions (read aloud / shown before the first trial)

You will hear pairs of short song excerpts, **clip A** and **clip B**, sung by a synthetic
Mandarin voice over a simple piano/instrument backing. The backing sound is deliberately plain;
please do not judge the sound quality of the synthesizer.

Each trial has two parts.

**Part 1 — compare A and B.** Listen to both clips (as often as you like), then answer:

* *Which clip's MIDDLE section is longer?* Each clip runs through the same sequence of song
  sections (for example verse → chorus → verse). Judge the length of the middle one.
* *Which clip is in the higher key?* Which one sounds higher overall.
* *Which clip sings the words shown on screen?* Read the words, then decide which clip sings them
  in its middle section.

If you cannot hear a difference, choose **no difference / neither** — that is a valid answer,
not a failure. Then rate how musically plausible each clip is as a pop-song excerpt
(1 = not at all, 5 = completely).

**Part 2 — check an edit.** You will now be told which clip is the ORIGINAL and which is the
EDITED version, and what the edit was supposed to do (e.g. "the middle section should be 4 bars
longer"). Answer:

* *Did the edited clip carry out the requested edit?* yes / partly / no
* *Apart from the requested edit, did the rest of the music stay the same?*
  1 = completely different … 5 = identical

A few trials are checks with an obvious answer; please answer every trial as carefully as the
others. There are no right answers in the plausibility ratings. The session takes about
45 minutes; take a break whenever you want.
